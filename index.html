01 / WHO WE ARE

A FOUNDATION BUILT
ON ENGINEERING.

Established in 2019, Brother’s Engineering & Construction is a Chattogram-based engineering and construction business focused on architectural design, structural design, interior design, consultancy and construction work.

Since its establishment, BEC has been developing its business through offline operations and practical involvement in the local construction industry. Now, as the company expands its online presence, we aim to make our services easier to discover and our approach clearer to clients planning their next project.

Led by Engr. Md. Shahidul Islam (Milad), a Civil Engineer holding a B.Sc. Engg. (Civil) qualification and FIEB credentials, BEC brings an engineering-led perspective to the planning and execution of building projects.

Our approach is simple: understand the client's requirements, consider the practical realities of the project, and work toward a solution that balances design, functionality, construction requirements and budget.

Whether the project begins with an idea, a plot of land, an architectural drawing or an existing property that needs improvement, BEC aims to provide the guidance and services needed to move it forward.

06 / CONSTRUCTION RIGOR

GOOD DESIGN IS ONLY THE BEGINNING.

A beautiful drawing is not a finished building.

The real test begins on site. Our construction approach focuses on proper execution, coordination, material quality, workmanship and attention to the details that determine how a building performs over time.

[FIELD EXECUTION · CHATTOGRAM]

STRUCTURAL CASTING & FIELD SUPERVISION

Real engineering happens in the field. From column reinforcement ties to foundation casting, our civil engineers maintain direct on-site presence to ensure BNBC compliance, structural safety, and lasting quality.

[STANDARD_01]

BNBC COMPLIANCE & SEISMIC RESILIENCE

Engineered according to Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC) criteria with appropriate seismic analysis and wind load provisions for Chattogram’s coastal environment.

[STANDARD_02]

MATERIALS VERIFICATION & BATCH TESTING

Use of verified structural steel rebar and tested Portland cement batches. Critical concrete casting phases are supported by standard slump and cylinder crush testing.

[STANDARD_03]

ON-SITE CIVIL SUPERVISION

Attentive engineer supervision ensures formwork alignment, rebar spacing, vibration consolidation, and water-curing schedules are executed properly.
