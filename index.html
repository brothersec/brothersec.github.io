06 / CONSTRUCTION RIGOR

GOOD DESIGN IS ONLY THE BEGINNING.

A beautiful drawing is not a finished building.

The real test begins on site. Our construction approach focuses on proper execution, coordination, material quality, workmanship and attention to the details that determine how a building performs over time.

[STANDARD_01]

BNBC 2020 & SEISMIC RESILIENCE

Engineered in consultation with senior structural consultants to withstand Zone 2 seismic forces and coastal wind loads according to BNBC 2020 criteria.

[STANDARD_02]

MATERIALS & BATCH TESTING

Only certified 60/72.5-grade steel rebar and tested Portland composite cement batches. Every concrete casting phase is backed by standard cylinder crush testing.

[STANDARD_03]

ON-SITE SUPERVISION

Continuous on-site engineer presence ensures shuttering alignment, rebar spacing, vibration consolidation, and water-curing schedules are strictly enforced.
